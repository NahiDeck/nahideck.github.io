NahiDeck Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 25, 2026

NahiDeck ("we", "us", or "the app") values the privacy of its users and the security of personal data. This Privacy Policy covers the entire NahiDeck ecosystem, including the NahiDeck Android Mobile Application, the NahiDeck Windows Desktop Client, and the NahiDeck Web Browser Extension (NahiDeck Connect).

2. Architecture & Local Communication (Zero Cloud Dependency)

NahiDeck operates primarily via direct peer-to-peer communication between your Android phone and Windows computer over local Wi-Fi, direct USB cable, or Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE):

  • Media & Audio Controls: Key presses, volume adjustments, and media control commands are exchanged strictly across your local network and never sent to cloud servers.
  • Desktop Macros & Scripts: Custom hotkeys and actions executed on your PC are processed locally by the desktop client.
  • Encryption: Local communication is protected end-to-end using ephemeral or self-signed TLS certificates with TOFU (Trust On First Use) certificate fingerprint pinning.

4. Cloud Backup (Google Drive — Independent Section)

NahiDeck offers an optional cloud backup feature for screen layouts, profiles, and custom user preferences:

  • Storage Location: Cloud backups are stored exclusively in the user's private Google Drive AppData folder (drive.appdata). Backup files are never uploaded to, hosted on, or accessed by NahiDeck servers or third parties.
  • Backup Contents: Screen placements, user profiles, theme options, and optional PC action scripts. Device secrets, authentication passwords, and network tokens are strictly excluded.
  • Separation from Telemetry: Cloud backup is completely segregated from telemetry. Backup content is never read or aggregated for analytic purposes.

5. Data We Never Collect (Zero Violation Policy)

  • Location Data: GPS or coarse/fine location services are never accessed. (Android BLE scan permissions are strictly utilized for local peer discovery).
  • Personal Files: Photos, videos, local documents, or voice recordings are never accessed.
  • Contacts: Phonebook and contact lists are never accessed.
  • Hardware Identifiers: IMEI, MAC addresses, or persistent hardware serials are never collected; ephemeral synthetic UUIDs are used instead.

6. Retention and Deletion

  • Account & License Data: Deleted permanently upon request via Settings > Account > "Delete Account" in the mobile app, or via our online Account Deletion page.
  • Telemetry Records: Revoking consent in Settings > Privacy immediately triggers cloud deletion of the telemetry record. Active records automatically expire after 180 days (TTL).
  • Technical Server Logs: Retained for a maximum of 30 days for operational integrity and security auditing.

7. Your Rights (GDPR & Data Protection)

Under GDPR and applicable privacy legislation, you have the right to access, rectify, port, or erase your personal data held in our authentication and licensing systems. To exercise these rights, please contact our support desk at nahideck.app@gmail.com.
