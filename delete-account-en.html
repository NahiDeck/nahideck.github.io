Account & Data Deletion Request

At NahiDeck, we respect your right to privacy and data control. If you wish to permanently delete your NahiDeck account and all associated data, you may follow either of the procedures outlined below.

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Recommended)

The quickest way to erase your data immediately is directly within the mobile application:

  1. Open the NahiDeck app on your Android device.
  2. Navigate to the Settings screen via the navigation dock (or top bar menu).
  3. Open the Account section.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete My Account".
  5. Confirm the action in the prompt.
  6. Upon confirmation, your Firebase user record, active paired devices, and cloud license documents will be erased immediately and irreversibly.

Note: Even if you delete your account, your app backup in Google Drive will not be deleted automatically. You must delete the backup yourself from Google Drive settings (Google Drive > Settings > Manage Apps).

Method 2: Web & Email Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the application or cannot access your device, you can submit an account deletion request via email:

Support Email: nahideck.app@gmail.com

Email Subject: NahiDeck Account and Data Deletion Request

Required Details:

  • The Google Account Email Address used to authenticate with NahiDeck.
  • (Optional) Google Play order number or RevenueCat subscription identifier, if applicable.

Once identity verification is confirmed, your account and all associated cloud records will be permanently removed within 7 business days.

What Data Is Permanently Deleted?

  • Firebase Authentication user profile, email address, and authentication tokens.
  • Pro license document under the Firestore entitlements collection.
  • Paired hardware device records under the Firestore devices collection.
  • RevenueCat subscriber profile (app_user_id).
  • Local secure storage tokens and hardware pairing secrets.

What Data Is Retained?

  • Google Play Billing Records: Historical transaction and invoice records are held directly by Google Inc. in accordance with legal and financial compliance requirements; NahiDeck does not host payment details.
  • Infrastructure Security Logs: Anonymous audit and security logs without personal attribution expire automatically within 90 days (TTL).

Important: Please Cancel Active Store Subscriptions

Deleting your NahiDeck account does not automatically cancel active recurring subscriptions managed via Google Play (per Google Play developer guidelines, third-party apps cannot cancel customer store subscriptions on their behalf).

To prevent future billing, please cancel your subscription prior to deleting your account:

  1. Open the Google Play Store app on Android.
  2. Tap your profile avatar > Payments & Subscriptions > Subscriptions.
  3. Locate NahiDeck and select "Cancel Subscription".
